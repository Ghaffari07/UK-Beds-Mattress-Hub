What changes when you order from us

Assembled where it lands

Our own two-person crews build the frame and place the mattress in the room you choose — up two flights of stairs if that's what it takes.

100 nights, not 30

Most bodies take a few weeks to adjust to a new mattress. We give you over three months before asking if it's right.

Old bed taken away

Tell us to clear your old mattress or frame on delivery day and it leaves with our van — recycled where it can be, no extra charge.

Same-week UK dispatch

Message us and most orders leave the workshop within the week — free delivery anywhere in the UK, no exceptions.

Made close to home, tested on real beds

UK Beds Mattress Hub started as a single workshop in Harrogate in 2011, upholstering bed frames for local hotels that had grown tired of imported furniture falling apart within a year. We still cut every frame within the UK, and every mattress design is slept on by our own team — not just modelled on a spec sheet — before it reaches a customer's bedroom.
